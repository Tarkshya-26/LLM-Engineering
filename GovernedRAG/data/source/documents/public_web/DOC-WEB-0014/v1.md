# Insurellm Launches Claimllm

SAN FRANCISCO: Insurellm today announced the general availability of Claimllm, aI-powered claims processing platform across all insurance lines.

"Claimllm brings the same AI platform our customers trust to a new part of the insurance value chain," said Avery Lancaster, founder and CEO of Insurellm.
