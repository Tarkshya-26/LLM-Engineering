# Contract with Crestview Re for Rellm

**Contract Date:** August 11, 2025
**Contract Number:** RE-2025-0335

## Terms

- **Parties Involved:** This contract is entered into between Insurellm, Inc. ("Provider") and Crestview Reinsurance Ltd. ("Client").
- **License Grant:** Insurellm grants Crestview Re a non-exclusive, non-transferable license to use the Rellm Professional Plan platform.
- **Payment Terms:** Crestview Re agrees to pay £8,000 per month for the 24 month term, totaling £192,000, on net 30 terms.
- **Term:** Effective August 11, 2025 through August 10, 2027.
- **User Licenses:** 20 named users, with training for 10 staff members.

## Renewal

- **Automatic Renewal:** The contract renews for successive 12 month terms unless either party gives 60 days' written notice.
- **Pricing Review:** Renewal price increases are limited to 5% per year.
- **Termination:** Either party may terminate with 60 days' written notice.

## Support

- **Support Level:** Standard support through the Insurellm customer portal and email.
