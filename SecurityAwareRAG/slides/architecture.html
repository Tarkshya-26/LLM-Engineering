what broke during development

Three findings I kept.

01

My corpus was contaminated

Every attack document ended with a disclaimer telling the model to ignore it. The baseline looked secure because the attack was announcing itself.

02

My detector had false negatives — twice

Both errors flattered the system. One scored "classified as LOW risk" as blocked. Caught by disbelieving a result, not by a test.

03

Context isolation did not reduce prompt injection

The layer I was most confident about. Matched runs, N=12: 3/12 → 4/12. Structural isolation removes privilege, not persuasion.
