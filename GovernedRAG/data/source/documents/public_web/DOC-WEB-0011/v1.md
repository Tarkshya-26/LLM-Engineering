# Insurellm Announces Agreement With GlobalRe Partners

SAN FRANCISCO: Insurellm announced a 48 month agreement with GlobalRe Partners, which will use Rellm across its operations.

"We chose Rellm because it lets our teams move faster without compromising on control," said a spokesperson for GlobalRe Partners.

"GlobalRe Partners is exactly the kind of partner we built Rellm for," said Avery Lancaster, founder and CEO of Insurellm.

Financial terms were not disclosed.
