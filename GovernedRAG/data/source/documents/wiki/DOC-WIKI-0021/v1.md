# On-call handbook

Applies to all production systems.

## Steps
- Keep your phone reachable during the shift.
- Hand over open issues at the end of the shift.
- Escalate early when unsure.
- Never share customer data in public channels.

## Contacts
- Engineering on-call through the paging system.
- Security for any suspected data exposure.
