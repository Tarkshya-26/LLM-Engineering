# Insurellm Announces Agreement With Metropolitan Life Group

SAN FRANCISCO: Insurellm announced a 36 month agreement with Metropolitan Life Group, which will use Lifellm across its operations.

"We chose Lifellm because it lets our teams move faster without compromising on control," said a spokesperson for Metropolitan Life Group.

"Metropolitan Life Group is exactly the kind of partner we built Lifellm for," said Avery Lancaster, founder and CEO of Insurellm.

Financial terms were not disclosed.
