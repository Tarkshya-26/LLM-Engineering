# Contract with Harborline Assurance for Homellm

**Contract Date:** August 1, 2025
**Contract Number:** HM-2025-0334

## Terms

- **Parties Involved:** This contract is entered into between Insurellm, Inc. ("Provider") and Harborline Assurance, Inc. ("Client").
- **License Grant:** Insurellm grants Harborline Assurance a non-exclusive, non-transferable license to use the Homellm Standard Tier platform.
- **Payment Terms:** Harborline Assurance agrees to pay $10,000 per month for the 24 month term, totaling $240,000, on net 30 terms.
- **Term:** Effective August 1, 2025 through July 31, 2027.
- **User Licenses:** 20 named users, with training for 10 staff members.

## Renewal

- **Automatic Renewal:** The contract renews for successive 12 month terms unless either party gives 60 days' written notice.
- **Pricing Review:** Renewal price increases are limited to 5% per year.
- **Termination:** Either party may terminate with 60 days' written notice.

## Support

- **Support Level:** Standard support through the Insurellm customer portal and email.
