# Product Roadmap

## Markellm
- **2025-Q1:** Launch a mobile app version of Markellm, making it even easier for consumers and insurers to connect on-the-go
- **2025-Q1:** Introduce a referral program that rewards users for promoting Markellm to their network
- **2025-Q2:** Expand the marketplace to include additional insurance products, such as life and health insurance
- **2025-Q2:** Partner with third-party data aggregators to enhance the accuracy of our AI matching capabilities
- **2025-Q3:** Initiate a comprehensive marketing campaign targeting both consumers and insurers to increase user acquisition and brand awareness
- **2025-Q3:** Release user testimonials and case studies showcasing successful matches made through Markellm
- **2026-Q4:** Implement machine learning enhancements to our AI algorithm, further increasing the precision and personalization of matches
- **2026-Q4:** Explore international expansion opportunities, launching in select markets outside the US

## Carllm
- **2025-Q1:** Expanded data integrations for better risk assessment
- **2025-Q1:** Enhanced fraud detection algorithms to reduce losses
- **2025-Q2:** Launch of a new mobile app for end-users
- **2025-Q2:** Introduction of telematics-based pricing to provide even more tailored coverage options
- **2025-Q3:** Begin pilot programs for international insurance markets
- **2025-Q3:** Collaborate with local insurers to offer compliant, localized versions of Carllm
- **2025-Q4:** Implement next-gen machine learning models for predictive analysis
- **2025-Q4:** Roll out customer insights dashboard updates based on user feedback
- **2026:** Increase partnerships with automakers for integrated insurance solutions
- **2026:** Enhance the AI customer support system to include multi-language support

## Homellm
- **2024-Q1:** Launch of Homellm version 1.0, featuring core functionalities and integrations
- **2024-Q3:** Introduction of enhanced analytics capabilities, including visualization tools and advanced reporting features
- **2025-Q1:** Release of Homellm version 2.0, with expanded predictive maintenance alerts and automated underwriting processes
- **2025-Q3:** Establish partnerships with IoT device manufacturers to provide integrated solutions for proactive risk management
- **2026-Q1:** Ongoing improvements based on user feedback and industry trends, ensuring that Homellm remains at the forefront of home insurance technology

## Rellm
- **2025-Q3:** Launch of the Rellm Mobile App for on-the-go insights and management
- **2025-Q3:** Introduction of augmented reality (AR) features for interactive risk assessments
- **2026-Q1:** Deployment of advanced machine learning models for even more accurate risk predictions
- **2026-Q1:** Expansion of integration capabilities to support emerging technologies in the insurance sector
- **2026-Q3:** Release of a community platform for Rellm users to exchange insights, tips, and best practices
- **2026-Q3:** Launch of Rellm 2.0, featuring enhanced user interface and premium features based on user feedback

## Lifellm
- **2025-Q2:** Launch of Lifellm version 1.0 with core AI underwriting and policy management capabilities
- **2025-Q4:** Introduction of digital health platform integrations with major wearable device manufacturers and health app providers
- **2026-Q2:** Release of Lifellm version 2.0 featuring enhanced predictive modeling, genetic risk assessment capabilities, and blockchain-based policy verification
- **2026-Q4:** Launch of embedded life insurance API allowing partners to offer life coverage within their own platforms and applications
- **2027-Q2:** Expansion into international markets with localized regulatory compliance features and multi-currency support

## Healthllm
- **2025-Q1:** Launch of Healthllm version 1.0 featuring core claims processing, eligibility verification, and member portal
- **2025-Q3:** Introduction of predictive analytics module for population health management and risk stratification
- **2026-Q1:** Release of advanced AI claims adjudication with automated fraud detection and payment optimization
- **2026-Q3:** Launch of social determinants of health (SDOH) integration, enabling holistic member care and targeted interventions
- **2027-Q1:** Introduction of value-based care management tools supporting ACO and bundled payment models
- **2027-Q3:** Global expansion with international healthcare system integrations and multi-language support

## Bizllm
- **2025-Q2:** Launch of Bizllm version 1.0 with core multi-line underwriting, quoting, and policy administration
- **2025-Q4:** Introduction of enhanced cyber insurance capabilities including active threat monitoring and breach response coordination
- **2026-Q2:** Release of embedded IoT integration for real-time risk monitoring in property and workers' comp lines
- **2026-Q4:** Launch of parametric insurance module enabling rapid payout triggers for weather events and business interruption
- **2027-Q2:** Introduction of climate risk modeling tools incorporating forward-looking environmental data and transition risk analysis
- **2027-Q4:** Expansion into international markets with country-specific commercial insurance products and regulatory compliance

## Claimllm
- **2025-Q1:** Launch of Claimllm version 1.0 with core claims processing automation, FNOL capture, and basic fraud detection
- **2025-Q3:** Introduction of advanced computer vision capabilities for property and auto damage assessment with repair cost estimation
- **2026-Q1:** Release of predictive litigation analytics identifying claims likely to involve attorneys and recommending settlement strategies
- **2026-Q3:** Launch of blockchain-based subrogation network enabling automated inter-carrier claim recovery and settlement
- **2027-Q1:** Introduction of real-time settlement negotiation AI that optimizes settlement amounts based on claim details and historical outcomes
- **2027-Q3:** Expansion into catastrophe claims management with disaster response coordination and large-loss handling capabilities
