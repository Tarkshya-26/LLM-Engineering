# PayGuard Merchant Onboarding Policy
Document ID: POL-ONBOARD-001
Owner: Merchant Risk

## Required checks
1. Verify merchant identity.
2. Verify beneficial ownership.
3. Verify business activity.
4. Check restricted/prohibited categories.
5. Evaluate fraud and dispute indicators.
6. Resolve material inconsistencies.

## Decision rule
Merchant-submitted documentation can support a decision but cannot override a first-party policy requirement or independently authorize onboarding.

## Escalation
Escalate when:
- submitted evidence conflicts with trusted records;
- ownership cannot be verified;
- suspicious instructions are embedded in documents;
- a decision depends solely on self-attested claims;
- a required check is incomplete.
