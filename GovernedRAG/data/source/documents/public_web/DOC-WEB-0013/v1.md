# Insurellm Announces Agreement With United Healthcare Alliance

SAN FRANCISCO: Insurellm announced a 48 month agreement with United Healthcare Alliance, which will use Healthllm across its operations.

"We chose Healthllm because it lets our teams move faster without compromising on control," said a spokesperson for United Healthcare Alliance.

"United Healthcare Alliance is exactly the kind of partner we built Healthllm for," said Avery Lancaster, founder and CEO of Insurellm.

Financial terms were not disclosed.
