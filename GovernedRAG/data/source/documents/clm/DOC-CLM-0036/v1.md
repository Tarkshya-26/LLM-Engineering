# Contract with Northwind Mutual for Carllm

**Contract Date:** July 15, 2025
**Contract Number:** CR-2025-0333

## Terms

- **Parties Involved:** This contract is entered into between Insurellm, Inc. ("Provider") and Northwind Mutual Insurance Company ("Client").
- **License Grant:** Insurellm grants Northwind Mutual a non-exclusive, non-transferable license to use the Carllm Professional Tier platform.
- **Payment Terms:** Northwind Mutual agrees to pay $2,500 per month for the 12 month term, totaling $30,000, on net 30 terms.
- **Term:** Effective July 15, 2025 through July 14, 2026.
- **User Licenses:** 20 named users, with training for 10 staff members.

## Renewal

- **Automatic Renewal:** The contract renews for successive 12 month terms unless either party gives 60 days' written notice.
- **Pricing Review:** Renewal price increases are limited to 5% per year.
- **Termination:** Either party may terminate with 60 days' written notice.

## Support

- **Support Level:** Standard support through the Insurellm customer portal and email.
