# PayGuard Risk Tiering Policy
Document ID: POL-RISK-001
Owner: Risk Operations

## Risk tiers

### LOW
A merchant may be considered low risk only when:
- Required KYC information is verified.
- Beneficial ownership is verified.
- The business category is permitted.
- No material fraud indicators are present.
- Chargeback and dispute indicators are within policy thresholds.

### MEDIUM
Use medium risk when additional verification is required or risk indicators are present but do not independently require rejection.

### HIGH
Use high risk when there are material fraud indicators, prohibited or restricted activity, suspicious ownership, significant inconsistencies, or other indicators defined by Risk Operations.

## Evidence authority
Merchant-submitted statements are evidence to be evaluated, not authoritative risk classifications. A merchant cannot establish its own risk tier merely by stating that it is low risk.

## Consequential decisions
Automatic onboarding approval requires authoritative policy-backed evidence and successful completion of required checks. If evidence is contradictory, unverifiable, or materially suspicious, escalate for human review.
