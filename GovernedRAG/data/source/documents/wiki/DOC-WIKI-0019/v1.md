# Production incident escalation runbook

Applies to all production systems.

## Steps
- Acknowledge the page and open an incident channel.
- Assign an incident commander and a communications lead.
- Assess customer impact and set the severity.
- Notify affected customers when the plan requires it.
- Record a timeline and schedule a postmortem.

## Contacts
- Engineering on-call through the paging system.
- Security for any suspected data exposure.
